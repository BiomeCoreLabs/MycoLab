🔌 Hardware Integration Guides

1. Pairing Tuya Smart Plugs (LAN & Cloud)

MycoLabs supports direct local subnet socket communication with Tuya/Smart Life plugs without routing commands through remote cloud servers:

  • Ensure your iPhone/iPad is connected to the same 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network as the Tuya smart plugs.
  • In MycoLabs, open Settings > Hardware & Sensors > Tuya IoT QuickDock.
  • Enter your Tuya Developer credentials (Access ID & API Secret) for automatic local token discovery. Once tokens are synchronized, MycoLabs communicates over direct local UDP/TCP.
  • Assign channel sockets: Humidifier (Mist Pulse), Exhaust Fan (FAE), and Photoperiod Lighting (Circadian 6500K).

2. 24/7 Chamber Camera Setup via LiveKit WebRTC

Turn any old iPad, iPhone, or Mac into a dedicated grow chamber camera:

  • Open MycoLabs on the camera device and navigate to Camera > Broadcast Mode.
  • Open MycoLabs on your primary device, go to the Dashboard, tap Living Grow Camera Card > Scan Pairing Code.
  • The stream establishes an encrypted, sub-second peer-to-peer WebRTC connection.
  • To activate automated timelapse rendering, toggle Timelapse Mode and specify your capture interval (e.g. 1 frame every 15 minutes).

3. Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) Calibration

MycoLabs uses standard Tetens and Arden Buck psychrometric equations. For best accuracy:

  • Position your thermo-hygrometer at canopy height, away from direct mist nozzles or exhaust drafts.
  • If testing dry-bulb temperature, ensure the sensor probe has acclimated for at least 30 minutes inside the chamber.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is an Internet connection required during daily operation?

No. MycoLabs is built strictly local-first. All telemetry processing, SQLite databases, biometric locks, and local Tuya LAN sockets execute directly on your local network. An Internet connection is only required if you explicitly use Tuya Cloud as a fallback outside your home network.

How do I reset or exit Demo Mode?

If you activated Demo Mode during onboarding, an amber banner ("🧪 Demo Modus Actief") appears at the top of your Dashboard. Tap this banner at any time to clear the demo data and return to a pristine blank slate.

Which mushroom species are supported in cultivation presets?

In strict accordance with Apple Guideline 1.4.3, MycoLabs includes optimized environmental regimes exclusively for gourmet culinary and functional medicinal species: Lion's Mane (Hericium erinaceus), Blue/Golden/King Oyster (Pleurotus ostreatus / eryngii), Shiitake (Lentinula edodes), Reishi (Ganoderma lucidum), and Pioppino.
