Privacy Policy for MycoLabs

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Published by BiomeCore Labs

The BiomeCore Labs Privacy Pledge

At BiomeCore Labs, we believe biological telemetry and proprietary culture records belong strictly to the researcher and cultivator. MycoLabs was engineered with a 100% Local-First Architecture. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or inspect your personal or laboratory data.

1. Local-First Data Storage & Zero Telemetry

Cultivation Records, Genetics Lineage & Media

All strain information, batch notes, agar dish photos, 4K timelapses, and harvest dry weight calculations are stored exclusively inside your device's sandboxed local application directory (AppDatabase). BiomeCore Labs operates zero telemetry collection servers.

Zero Advertising SDKs & Zero Tracking

MycoLabs contains no third-party analytics trackers, no Facebook/Google SDKs, and does not request or track Apple's IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers).

Apple Privacy Manifest Compliance

In accordance with Apple's declared API reasons guidelines (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy):

  • CA92.1 (File Timestamp APIs): Used exclusively to verify local database and timelapse cache integrity.
  • C617.1 (System Boot Time): Used solely to schedule local background telemetry evaluation via BGTaskScheduler.
  • 35F9.1 (Disk Space): Used to ensure sufficient storage before exporting uncompressed ProRes timelapse video sequences.

2. Hardware & Device Permissions

Camera Permission

Used strictly on-device to scan laboratory QR codes attached to culture jars and to stream local video when configured as a chamber camera.

Microphone Permission

Used exclusively when tapping the Cleanroom Voice Assistant for sterile glove operation. Speech recognition is evaluated locally using on-device neural models and is never transmitted off your device.

Local Network Permission

Used strictly to discover and issue commands to Tuya smart plugs and Raspberry Pi camera hubs over your home Wi-Fi subnet.
