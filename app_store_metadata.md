MycoLabs is the definitive laboratory operating system for gourmet and medicinal mushroom cultivators. Engineered for precision, privacy, and modularity, MycoLabs transforms any iPhone, iPad, or Mac into a complete smart cultivation workstation.

KEY CAPABILITIES:

• LIVE TELEMETRY & CLIMATE AUTOMATION:
Track temperature, relative humidity (% RH), and real-time thermodynamic Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD). Automatically calculate optimal fruiting conditions using precision psychrometric equations.

Modular architecture adapts to any setup: Monotubs, Martha tents, fruiting rooms, or bags. Connect smart relays via local Tuya LAN, MQTT, Home Assistant, ESP32, or log observations 100% manually.

• 24/7 ENCRYPTED CHAMBER CAMERA & TIMELAPSE:
Turn any spare iOS device or Mac into an ultra-low latency WebRTC chamber camera node. Automatically capture scheduled growth time-lapses across multi-day flushes with direct export to Apple Photos.

• HANDS-FREE STERILE VOICE CONSOLE:
Maintain sterile cleanroom protocols while wearing gloves. Dictate harvest logs, trigger misting cycles, and inspect climate metrics using local, on-device neural speech recognition.

• GENETICS REGISTRY & ANCESTRAL LINEAGE:
Map your genetic library across generations. Trace spore prints, liquid cultures, and agar transfers (P1 through P4) with visual family lineage trees.

• PRECISION SUBSTRATE & YIELD CALCULATORS:
Scale field-capacity CVG (Coco Coir, Vermiculite, Gypsum) substrate batches, Master's Mix hardwood blends, and calculate Biological Efficiency (BE%) yield ratios.

• SOVEREIGN PRIVACY & LOCAL-FIRST STORAGE:
Zero tracking. Zero telemetry. No mandatory cloud accounts. All laboratory logs remain securely sandboxed on your device with optional Face ID / Touch ID biometric protection.

Designed with scientific rigor by BiomeCore Labs.
