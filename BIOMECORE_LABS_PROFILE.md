## 🏛️ Our Core Principles

### 1. 🛡️ Sovereign & Local-First Architecture
We reject unnecessary cloud lock-in, forced subscriptions, and ubiquitous surveillance telemetry. Whenever possible, our software runs directly on your hardware, stores data locally in sandboxed databases, and functions 100% offline.

### 2. ⚡ Native Performance & Craftsmanship
We build natively with **Swift 6, SwiftUI, Metal, and low-level system APIs**. No bloated cross-platform web wrappers, no sluggish interfaces. Every frame, micro-interaction, and animation is designed to feel instantaneous and fluid (120Hz ProMotion).

### 3. 🔌 Hardware & Physical World Synergy
We love the intersection of software and physical reality: local Wi-Fi protocols, WebRTC media streams, embedded microcontrollers (ESP32), smart sensors, and automated actuators.

### 4. 🎨 High-Contrast, Focused Aesthetics
Our design language combines dark, high-contrast visual hierarchies with intuitive tactile controls. Complex data and controls are structured with clarity and precision.
